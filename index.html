What I bring

  1. HW

    Hardware & install

    Rooms built from scratch: rack, cable, lighting, speakers, displays, network. Platform-agnostic, focused on the technology underneath any one brand.

    AV over IP · managed switching, VLANs, multicast, QoS · speaker prototyping · digital amplification
  2. SW

    Software & interfaces

    Mobile-first control panels and apps, designed on a phone for people who use a phone. Fixed buttons, colour per room, no hidden gestures.

    UI/UX for AV & home control · Progressive Web Apps · “Tap ’n Go” design doctrine
  3. LG

    Logistics & live shows

    From concert gear prep in the early ’90s to running my own rundowns: switcher, live mix, PTZ cameras and streaming out. I lead crews and contractors on bigger builds.

    Video switching · live audio mixing · PTZ · streaming · crew coordination
  4. AI

    Working with Claude

    I direct, the AI operates. I bring intent, UX and field experience; Claude writes the code. Every project below was built this way.

    Human directs · AI operates · Ten Laws of AI governance
